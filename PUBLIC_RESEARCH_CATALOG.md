# Invariant Computing Lab — Public Research Software Catalogue

**Updated:** 8 November 2026 · **Public repositories:** 56

Invariant Computing Lab is an independent research programme founded by Emirhan Yıldırım. This catalogue indexes public source, computational studies and reproducibility artefacts associated with the Lab, grouped by research programme or technical role. It does not imply that all repositories represent independent studies, tested releases, peer-reviewed publications, licensed open-source products, clinical systems or validated physical models.

**Evidence boundaries.** Every repository must be interpreted according to its own `README.md`, source manifests, test conditions and any repository-specific rights files. Software-only PASS, frozen source hashes and synthetic evaluation results are not biological, clinical or physical validation. A public repository is not automatically permission to reuse copyrighted code, datasets or weights.

## Reading the catalogue responsibly

- Grouping is an editorial navigation aid, not a claim of separate institutional entities or separate scientific results.
- Several bounded `gXX` packages belong to larger source lineages and share upstream evidence. Count packages separately from publications and experiments.
- Private repositories, unreviewed data, internal research notes and unverified IP claims are deliberately excluded.
- For DOI and version metadata, use the publisher/archival record rather than inferring dates or status from GitHub.
- If a repository lacks an explicit licence, do **not** assume an open-source licence or redistribution permission.

**Contact and editorial corrections:** [Research portfolio](https://emirhanyildirim-cloud.github.io/). Claims and metadata are subject to provenance-led revision.
