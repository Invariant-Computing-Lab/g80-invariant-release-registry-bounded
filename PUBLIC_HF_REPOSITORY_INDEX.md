This source index distinguishes model weights, research datasets and interactive Spaces; these are not interchangeable evidence types. Visibility on the Hub does not grant source or model licences, establish independent testing, or demonstrate clinical validity. Read each repository's limitations, licence and files before reuse.

**Institutional scope:** Invariant Computing Lab covers foundational, finite and falsification-oriented research. Invariant Ventures contains separate application/prototype experiments. A Space may be a static demonstration; a model can be finite parameters rather than a language model; synthetic datasets do not constitute patient or biological validation.
