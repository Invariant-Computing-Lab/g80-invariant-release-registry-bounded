## Why this is a priority

Several public Zenodo records associated with Invariant Computing Lab show the depositor's name as **"Yıldırım, Emirhan (Related person)"** in an indexed `Authors/Creators` section. A public Zenodo page showing a creator role is a piece of metadata evidence; it is **not by itself a definitive determination** of who wrote the associated manuscript. In particular, **repository ownership, community curation, a PDF filename and an ORCID self-asserted work are distinct claims**.

## Review/repair protocol

1. For each record, open the actual manuscript or depositor-approved source package and read the **byline, author contribution and rights statements**. If the owner authored the work, compare with the live Zenodo `Creators` and `Contributors` sections.
2. **Correct the existing record's metadata in place only where the source confirms an error.** Zenodo permits editing title, creators and other metadata after publication without generating a new DOI; update the record and publish the metadata revision. Do **not** upload a duplicate record or new version solely to repair a creator role.
3. Where appropriate, attach ORCID `0009-0002-5884-3660` to the correct creator entry. Keep university affiliation factual; **Invariant Computing Lab is an independent research identity, not a university accreditation**.
4. Reload the public record and verify exact DOI, title, author/creator role, source attribution, licence and publication type. Save a dated audit receipt before changing ORCID Works.
5. Reconcile ORCID only after the Zenodo records and manuscript bylines agree. Preserve existing self-asserted ORCID entries and avoid duplicate DOI imports.
6. Keep original work statuses clear: *preprint, synthetic/computational evidence, unresolved experimental validation*. The Zenodo `Patent` resource type does not itself evidence a granted patent.

## What not to do

- Do **not** infer authorship merely because the same individual deposited the file or curates the community.
- Do **not** silently overwrite creator roles without checking source works, and do not assign open licences without rights evidence.
- Do **not** describe a submission as accepted, the model as clinically validated, or a patent application as granted without an independent primary record.
- Do **not** reuse invalid RARI identifier `21887971`; the verified version-specific DOI is `10.5281/zenodo.21875043`.
