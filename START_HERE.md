# Invariant Computing Lab — Start Here

*Independent computational research · reproducibility tools · falsifiable models*

Invariant Computing Lab is an independent research initiative founded by **Emirhan Yıldırım**. It develops computational frameworks and research software for questions about observer-limited inference, representation, identity under transformation and recoverability. The Lab separates **foundational hypotheses**, **finite or synthetic evidence**, **reproducibility artefacts** and **implementation ventures**.

We would rather show a negative result with its constraints than present a simulation as biological proof. A public preprint or repository is a traceable research output, not an independently replicated result.

## Three ways into the work

### 1. Researcher / scientist — read the claims and their failure cases

- **Neural-state recoverability (RARI):** [From Relational Invariants to Spectral Reconstructibility](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21875043) reports four falsification stages with public datasets, including negative cross-dataset generalisation and unresolved recovery prediction. These are **secondary computational analyses**, not evidence of brain-death reversal or a clinical biomarker.
- **Representational geometry (HuReT):** [A Falsifiable Geometric–Arithmetic Framework for Cognitive Computation](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21382709) tests task-conditional geometry with digit models and synthetic transformation tasks. Neural population validation remains a separate research requirement.
- **Relational identity under transformation:** [Dyadic Neural Systems](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21904855) studies quotient invariants and adversarial/common-cause failures in synthetic coupling models, not demonstrated shared consciousness.
- **Finite geometrogenesis model:** [FORGE-L](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21609625) gives an explicitly finite, falsifiable hypergraph rewrite framework, not a physically observed spacetime theory.

These are public research **records** (and may be preprints); community membership and a DOI do not confer peer review.

### 2. Developer / replication researcher — inspect source and evidence

- [56 public GitHub repositories grouped by research programme](PUBLIC_RESEARCH_CATALOG.md). Package count is **not** paper count.
- [File-tree release-readiness audit](REPOSITORY_TREE_READINESS_AUDIT.md): test-path, licence-path, dependency and CI *presence only*, not a run receipt.
- [58 public Hugging Face model, dataset and Space repositories](PUBLIC_HF_REPOSITORY_INDEX.md): models may be fitted finite numeric artefacts; Spaces may be demonstrations; synthetic datasets are not patient data.
- [DOI ↔ Zenodo community provenance](DOI_COMMUNITY_CROSSWALK.md). The record list is curated, not equivalent to the entire personal historical archive.

**Reproducibility standard:** version/frozen source, exact input fixtures, explicit environment, checksums, rejection tests, documented rights, and an independent rerun where feasible. Claims are only as strong as the documented test regime.

### 3. Collaborator / partner — understand what is and is not ready

**RARI, OCIL/OCIR, AFC, SACRA and related frameworks** are research programmes with different questions and levels of evidence. Related ventures under **Invariant Ventures**, including Humanization Tech and Invariant OS, are separate applied workstreams. A named programme or web page does not by itself prove a registered company, deployed product or clinical approval.

**FULMINA is not one completed neural device.** Three distinct lines must be evaluated separately:

1. **FULMINA-8:** computational alternatives and experiments proposed to discriminate physical mechanisms; modelling is not biological confirmation.
2. **Natural Intelligence (NI) chip / hardware:** CAD, electronics, firmware and human-approval/safety software planning; no independently verified physical prototype or live-tissue demonstration in the public evidence indexed here.
3. **Fulmina Circuit:** a distinct multiscale theory/computation manuscript; do not interpret a journal submission as acceptance. Current editorial status must be checked before any publication claim.

**Intellectual property:** patent application documentation and a trademark filing are application-stage evidence, not issued patent or registered-mark certificates. No ethics committee approval or clinical patient trial is claimed here.

## Before citing or reusing work

- Check the **exact version DOI**, not just a project title or concept DOI.
- Inspect the specific manuscript author list and source's contributor roles. An indexed Zenodo "Related person" role requires [creator-attribution review](ZENODO_ATTRIBUTION_REVIEW.md) before new ORCID author imports.
- Inspect repository rights and third-party licences. Public visibility ≠ permission to redistribute source data, models or code.
- A software test or synthetic benchmark is not physical, biological or clinical validation.
