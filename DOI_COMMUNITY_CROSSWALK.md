## Archive-wide scope and release boundaries

At the 8 November audit, the personal Zenodo upload inventory contained **144 published records and 6 unpublished drafts**. The 144 published records were classified on the owner's dashboard as **70 preprints, 67 records with the Zenodo “Patent” resource type, and 7 books**. A Zenodo resource-type field is not evidence that a patent is registered or granted. The six drafts must **not** be represented as published outputs or auto-published merely to increase counts.

The complete personal research archive is broader than the Lab's curated set; an item is not automatically a Lab output simply because the founder uploaded it. Additional community assignments must pass creator-role, original-source, rights and project-relevance checks.
